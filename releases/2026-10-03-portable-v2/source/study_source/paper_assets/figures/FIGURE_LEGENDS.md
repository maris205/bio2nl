# Figure legends

**Figure 1. Fixed-head English evaluation after protein-pair supervision.** (a) Fixed label-1 area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC), (b) balanced accuracy and (c) accuracy on the historically evaluated QQP cohort (39,893 pairs, 33,128 shared-endpoint components). EP and ES share English input blocks and total token budget, with natural versus shuffled protein respectively; EE has twice their English exposure. Each open circle is the mean of three fine-tuning repeats within one pretraining seed; horizontal marks and error bars are the mean and sample SD of the three pretraining-seed means. Grey diamonds show the deterministic source-selected BPE-token surface reference and fixed constant-label classifiers C0/C1; no seed uncertainty is assigned to them. The same source-selected classifier, label direction and decision rule are retained in English. Dashed lines mark AUC/balanced-accuracy 0.5. Axes have different displayed ranges. Neither a confidence interval nor a test of significance is shown.

**Figure 2. Paired English ranking differences.** The prespecified primary comparison is EP−ES; EP−EE and ES−EE are contextual comparisons because EE has double English exposure. Coloured shapes are the differences between FT-averaged scores at matched PT seeds 0, 1 and 2. Black marks/error bars denote mean and sample SD across those three paired differences; the dashed line denotes zero difference. Positive differences in all three primary-comparison groups are descriptive and do not establish statistical significance, adequate source competence or successful fixed-threshold classification.

**Figure 3. Source learning of the classifiers retained for transfer.** Fixed-checkpoint accuracy on (a) source train (8,044 pairs), (b) source validation (20,276) and (c) source test (20,854). All panels use the same classifier selected by minimum source-validation cross-entropy, with ties broken toward the earlier epoch; validation therefore includes selection bias. Open circles and intervals use the same three-PT-seed aggregation as Figure1. The BPE-token surface reference is fit on the same 8,044 source rows and selected on source validation only. The 50% dashed line is the constant-label accuracy on these balanced splits. These controls establish surface predictive signal, not a neural mechanism or structural-homology competence. Separate raw-residue construction baselines trained on 99,818 rows appear in Supplementary TableS1 rather than being pooled into this budget-matched figure.
